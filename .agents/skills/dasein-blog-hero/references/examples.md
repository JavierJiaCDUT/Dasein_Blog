# Reference Examples

## Market Review

Canonical asset:

`src/assets/blog/2025-market-review/G96Gz8WXUAApbsW.jpeg`

Reusable principles:

- a single dominant fact
- centered hierarchy
- black, white, and one orange accent
- strong negative space
- safe behavior under card cropping

Do not copy its exact composition for every article. It is the `editorial-data` reference for number-led or annual content.

## Context Engineering

Canonical asset:

`src/assets/blog/the-new-rules-of-context-engineering-for-claude-5-models/hero.png`

Reusable principles:

- one evidence-based statistic as the first visual
- supporting modules that reveal a second layer of meaning
- asymmetric editorial grid
- exact short text rather than the full title
- source credit kept visually subordinate

It is the primary reference for combining `editorial-data` with a simple system diagram.

## Selection rule

Use the first example when one fact can carry the article alone. Use the second when a fact needs a small architecture, process, or relationship diagram to express the article's real argument.
